
# Bundled Surfer

Surfer is developed by the Surfer contributors and distributed under EUPL-1.2.
Upstream source: https://gitlab.com/surfer-project/surfer
Bundled revision: 3ab79e7220f937cb3fadf1117f15d344e5f7e990 (0.7.0-dev).

UCAgent modifies the native translator to expose digital vector bits through
initially collapsed compound rows. The corresponding source module, integration
patch, rebuild script and EUPL-1.2 license are included in the installed package
under `ucagent/scripts/surfer/`. The upstream revision supplies the remaining
Surfer source and its locked dependencies. Rebuild instructions are in the
waveform developer documentation.
